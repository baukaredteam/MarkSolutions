## Decision details

**Question:** What rules govern combining documents (invoice + customs declaration, withdrawal + audit trail, etc.) for regulated submissions?

**Selected option:** Central versioned policy by product group and operation type. Zero-or-more document links per CodeOrder/ImportOperation. Drafts can be incomplete; final regulated submission requires policy-complete evidence.

### Policy

1. Use **central versioned policy** by product group and operation type.
2. Model **zero-or-more document links** for each CodeOrder/ImportOperation.
3. **Drafts can be incomplete**; final regulated submission requires policy-complete evidence.
4. **Exceptions** require: approver, reason, expiry, and audit.
5. **No customer-specific policy bypass** — policy applies uniformly.
