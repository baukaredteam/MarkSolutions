## Decision details

**Question:** What is the official tariff pricing model for MarkFlow billing — subscription/service plans as monthly line items, and industrial marking codes at what price per code?

**Selected option:** Hybrid billing model.

**Approver name:** [Delegated Project Owner]
**Approver role:** Project Owner (delegated authority from leadership review)

**Approval date:** 2026-08-22

**Authoritative source/version:** MarkFlow_delegated_decision_records_and_prompts.md, decision table, dated 2026-08-22

**Affected MF-REQ IDs:** MF-REQ-040, MF-REQ-041, MF-REQ-042, MF-REQ-043, MF-REQ-044, MF-REQ-045

**Implementation authorization scope:** W0-03+ billing tariff hardening, ledger invariant enforcement, invoice/payment flows

### Policy

1. **Subscription/service plans** are monthly line items (not per-code).
2. **Industrial marking codes** are **800 tiyn (8 KZT) per externally accepted code**.
3. **Historical mockup input** of 0.84 KZT is retained only for reference; **never change a posted accrual**.
4. A **tariff snapshot** includes: `serviceType`, `unit`, `amountTiyn`, `currency` (KZT), `effectiveInterval`, `tariffVersion`, and `approvalRecordRef`.
5. The ledger must **reserve against requested quantity**, **capture against accepted quantity**, and **release the remainder**.
6. Record **operator payable** separately from customer balance, platform revenue, and payout reconciliation.
7. **No automatic customer-fund transfer or payout is authorized.**

### Test/acceptance evidence required
- [ ] Billing tests pass with 800 tiyn tariff (MF-REQ-044)
- [ ] Tariff snapshot contains all required fields
- [ ] Ledger reserve/capture/release invariant holds
- [ ] Operator payable recorded separately
- [ ] No automatic payout in any code path
