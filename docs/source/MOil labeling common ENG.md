# Motor oil marking in Kazakhstan

##### Sole Operator for Product Marking and Traceability

### What Is Digital Marking?

The system of marking and traceability of goods belongs to the class of Track&Trace systems. The control of turnover is based on the Data Matrix digital identification code.

##### DATA MATRIX

Digital labeling is the application of a Data Matrix code (means of identification) to each item of merchandise

Each code includes: **QR- Code**

1. Product type
2. Unique identification number
3. Cryptographic protection

### Means of Identification

**Means of identification -**a unique sequence of symbols in machine-readable form, represented in the form of a bar code, or recorded on a radio frequency tag, or represented using another means (technology) of automatic identification.

#### Attribute composition of the marking code

v **Full name of goods;** v **Information on shelf life;** v **Information on trademark, circulation and conformity mark;** v **Information on producer and manufacturer of goods;** v **Goods code (GTIN);** v **State classifier code;** v **EAEU HS code;** v **GPC code;** v **Brand;** v **Product purpose;** v **Declaration of conformity;** v **Motor oil viscosity class;** v **Volume;** v **Unit of measurement volume**
